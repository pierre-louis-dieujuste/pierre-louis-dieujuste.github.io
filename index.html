About me

I am at the beginning of my journey toward becoming a professional data analyst. I started with SQL because it is the language used to ask questions of databases, and it is the foundation of most analysis work.

What motivates me is solving real problems with data: finding what the numbers say, checking it carefully, and explaining it in a way anyone can understand.

This portfolio grows with me. Each project below will be added only once it is finished, with the problem, the method and the results.
